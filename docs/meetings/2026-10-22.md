## Presented

### 1. Before and after

| | Before (v1.1 and earlier) | After (v1.2.1, since 2026-10-02) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipe | Run-2 TOP data/POWHEG fit, $\exp(0.0615 - 0.0005\,p_T)$ | TOP-23-008: NNLO QCD + NLO EW / POWHEG × 13 → 13.6 TeV factor |
| Applied to | reconstructed AK8 jet $p_T$, two leading jets | parton-level `isLastCopy` top and antitop |
| Event weight | $\sqrt{w_0 w_1}$ | $\sqrt{\mathrm{SF}(t)\,\mathrm{SF}(\bar t)}$ |
| Cap | none | each factor held at the edge of its fit range (2 TeV, 1 TeV) |
| Uncertainty | none | `toppt`: up = no reweighting, down = 2w − 1 |
| Mean $t\bar t$ weight | 0.75 | 0.92 |

Per-top weight, new / old: 0.94 / 0.87 at 400 GeV, 0.89 / 0.65 at 1 TeV, 0.75 / 0.39 at 2 TeV.

Both factors are taken unchanged from TOP-23-008,
[CMS AN-2024/019](https://cmsfence.cern.ch/alcm/note/show-note-details?noteID=CMS%20AN-2024%2F019)
section 5.7, eqs. (3)–(4). Nothing was fitted by us; holding each factor at its fit edge
and the 2w − 1 down variation are our choices.

### 2. Why it changed

1. The TOP PAG recommends the TOP-23-008 treatment for Run 3
   ([CMS Talk, October 2025](https://cms-talk.web.cern.ch/t/top-pt-reweighting-recommendations-for-run-3-analyses/133877)).
2. The old implementation broke the [TWiki](https://twiki.cern.ch/twiki/bin/viewauth/CMS/TopPtReweighting)
   rules: it used the reconstructed jet instead of the parton-level top, and it was uncapped
   (the TWiki caps the formula at its 500 GeV value, 0.83; a 2 TeV jet got 0.39).
3. The old weight had no uncertainty and no 13.6 TeV treatment.

### 3. What the blinded fit says

In the masked background-only fit, $t\bar t$ ends at 0.63–0.80 of the MC in the Pass
windows and 0.36–0.59 in Fail. `toppt` is pulled −1.55σ, past the doubled correction; the
high-pT band SF (−1.7σ) and the per-year $t\bar t$ rates move the same way.

## Open question

Does $t\bar t$ need a data residual on top of the TOP-23-008 recipe? Options: keep the
recipe with a per-year rate (working default), add the TWiki data/NNLO residual, no
reweighting with a larger uncertainty (Run-2 B2G-25-009 practice), or measure it in the
semileptonic $t\bar t$ tag-and-probe.
