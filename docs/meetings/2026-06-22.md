## Presented

### 1. Blinded expected limits for all three widths (2024)

Combined cen + fwd, TF 2×1, snapshot Asimov, 109.95 fb⁻¹. Plots: Brazil bands per width
and overlays of the median limit with two theory lines.

| Width | XSDB-Par / ratio-anchored | MadGraph topcolor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1% | 4.84 TeV | 4.25 TeV |
| 10% | 5.73 TeV | 5.09 TeV |
| 30% | 7.08 TeV | 5.92 TeV |

The 1% bands needed the snapshot-Asimov fix (the prefit-TF Asimov gave the collapsed
1.8–3.5 TeV bands and the old 5.32 TeV). The 10% and 30% limits needed per-mass
theory scaling of the signal.

### 2. Topcolor theory from MadGraph

Leptophobic topcolor (Harris–Jain Model IV) σ×B computed with MG5 2.9.27 + `topBSM_UFO`,
NNPDF3.1 LO, widths 1/10/30%, 1–9 TeV. σ×B grows with width, while the official "Par"
sample cross sections fall with width, so they are the wrong overlay. The MadGraph values
sit 3–5× below XSDB-Par at every width, so the absolute normalisation is a choice.

### 3. LHAPDF parton-luminosity cross-check

A generator-free estimate of the width ratios agrees with MadGraph to 25–40% and confirms
that the large 30%/1% ratio at high mass is physics. The 13 → 13.6 TeV cross-section
ratio is +9% at 1 TeV to +36% at 6 TeV.

### 4. Discussion: why the limit seemed to depend on the signal scale

The limit on σ×B does not depend on how $r = 1$ is defined. Numerical tolerances and the
asymptotic bands are in units of $r$, so a badly matched normalisation (1 pb at high mass,
1 fb at low mass) pushes $r_{95}$ outside the valid range. Scaling the signal per mass so
$r_{95} \sim 1$, building the Asimov from the masked-fit snapshot, and starting at
$r = 0$ with $r \geq 0$ fix it.
